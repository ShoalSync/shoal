# Trademarks

Shoal's code is free software under MIT OR Apache-2.0: use it, change it and share it as those
licences allow.

The name "Shoal Sync" and the Shoal logo are not covered by those licences. They identify the
official project, so the people who use it know what they are getting. A fork or a modified build
that others will use must have a name and logo of its own, though it may say that it is based on
Shoal. Describing this project by name, as in "works with Shoal Sync", is fine.

SHOAL alone is a registered US mark of another company (Reg. 8258111, classes 9 and 42), which is
why the project's name is "Shoal Sync" (ADR 0043).
